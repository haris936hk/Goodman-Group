### CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility)


- As a part of this sector, we know our responsibility for (CSR) as well, which is a very important component for society.

- We provide free-of-cost medicines, life-saving drugs, and medical equipment to government charitable hospitals. We also provide medication required in emergencies for patients.

- Our primary objective is to provide medicine at low prices. We participate in tenders and quote minimum prices to ensure it reaches the most vulnerable masses of society.

- We organize free medical camps in rural areas where health facilities are minimal. We provide free medicine, awareness regarding health and hygiene, and clean drinking water.
