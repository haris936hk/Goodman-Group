# Hero Orbit Redesign

## Context
Improve the homepage GSAP circle so it reads as a connected five-company portfolio, with a more deliberate cinematic scroll handoff into the portfolio chapter. Keep all company names directly visible and clickable, retain existing dark/light theme colors, and preserve the mobile circle-plus-link-grid layout. The center Goodman Group logo remains the focal point.

## Approach
1. In `src/app/page.tsx`, retain the canonical `companies.map` links, existing logo assets, and `data-orbit-ring` hooks. Separate the five links from the rotating decorative outer ring: place them in a stationary positioned overlay within `.hero-system`, preserving link order, accessible names, and the existing `.orbit-node-1`…`-5` positions. Keep the decorative `.orbit-outer` and `.orbit-inner` as independently animatable elements, with `aria-hidden="true"` on purely decorative layers and pips. Add only CSS-based concentric traces/ticks to those layers (no new image assets or company claims). Put a dedicated decorative transition element immediately after `.hero-system` inside `.hero` only if the ring-to-chapter fade cannot be expressed with existing pseudo-elements.
2. In `src/app/globals.css`, refine `.hero-system`, `.orbit`, `.orbit::before`, `.hero-glow`, `.group-core`, `.orbit-node`, and theme-scoped equivalents into graduated, high-contrast cyan/navy and blue/white rings. Use a restrained set of line weights/opacity steps and ticks rather than adding more glowing blobs or colored company logos. Keep the core readable in both themes and interactive names legible above moving decoration. Keep current responsive bounds and center alignment; at ≤640px preserve the decorative square diagram above a two-column grid of five links (with the final cell spanning both columns if it improves balance), ensuring labels cannot overlap the rings or exceed a 320px viewport. Scope new transition styling to `.hero` and `.portfolio-chapter`; do not alter other chapters.
3. In `src/components/scroll-experience.tsx`, replace the desktop-only outer-ring rotation that currently rotates the links with a single scrubbed GSAP timeline scoped to `[data-hero]`, active only at desktop `(min-width: 1081px)`. Animate decorative outer/inner rings with opposed modest rotations and scale/opacity expansion toward the hero exit; animate glow/core depth modestly, and let decorative rings fade as the portfolio chapter enters. Keep the five links stationary and fully opaque throughout their visible hero lifetime. Use transforms and opacity only; keep the existing lazy imports, `gsap.context`, `gsap.matchMedia` cleanup, motion-generation guard, Lenis synchronization, and reduced-motion bypass. At ≤1080px, retain an attractive fully static scroll state rather than introducing pinning or scroll hijack. Limit entry animation to elements that actually exist in the hero; avoid introducing hidden initial states that could leave links invisible without JS. Avoid new perpetual animation and new dependencies.

## Critical files & anchors

## Assumptions & contingencies
- Preserve the site's existing centered logo-first hero rather than adding new headline/copy; the requested improvement concerns the circle and its handoff.
- The desktop animation is a scroll-scrubbed opening/fading of decorative layers, not movement of company links or pinned content; mobile and reduced-motion presentations remain static. Move the mobile grid rules from `.orbit-outer` to the new stationary links overlay, and position the outer decorative ring in the square graphic at mobile widths.
